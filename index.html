New research · Aug 2026 arXiv:2608.02295

MechGeo: Autoformalizing and Proving Euclidean Geometry in Lean 4

Hao Shen, Junyu Guo, Tian Cui, Yuxuan Xiao, Lihong Zhi

A Mathlib-native agentic framework for faithful autoformalization and certified proof construction. MechGeo turns informal geometry into Lean, diagnoses broken statements with verified counterexamples, and combines geometric reasoning with kernel-checked symbolic computation.
